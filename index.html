~ Welcome to Our Barn! ~

Hi and welcome!! We are a small family rabbitry located in Vess Township and we have been raising rabbits right here on the home place for going on four generations. My grandmother started with two does and a borrowed buck and we have kept the line going ever since.

We raise Netherland Dwarfs, Mini Rex, Dutch, New Zealand Whites and Flemish Giants. All our stock is pedigreed, handled daily, and raised in a clean barn. Visitors are welcome by appointment. Please call first — we do not answer the door after dark.

~ Care Sheet ~

Rabbits need fresh water daily, good quality timothy hay, and a clean dry hutch out of the wind. A rabbit that is handled every day from a young age will be a friendly rabbit for life. Do not house two bucks together.

New owners always ask me how long they live. A well cared for rabbit will give you eight to twelve good years. The doe in hutch 44 came to this farm with my grandmother and has outlived every animal in this barn, so I will say that the answer depends on the rabbit.
